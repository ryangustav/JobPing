**Effective Date:** 2025

By installing or using JobPing ("the extension"), you agree to these Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please uninstall the extension.

## 1. What JobPing Does
JobPing periodically opens the LinkedIn job search URL you configure in a background tab, **inside your own logged-in browser session**, reads the details of the most recent job card (title, company, posting time and link) and shows you a Chrome notification. It does this at most once per hour, reads only one job card per check and never reads profiles or any other LinkedIn data.
