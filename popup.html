Before you start

  • JobPing isn't made by or affiliated with LinkedIn.
  • It opens your search in a background tab (once an hour at most) and reads the first job on it.
  • LinkedIn's User Agreement (section 8.2) doesn't allow extensions that scrape the site, so using this could get your account restricted.
  • Nothing leaves your browser.

Want to stay 100% official? LinkedIn has its own job alerts ("Set alert" on any search). Terms of Use · Privacy
